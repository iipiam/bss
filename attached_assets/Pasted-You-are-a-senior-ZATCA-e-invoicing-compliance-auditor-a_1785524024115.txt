**Mandatory Phase 2 Technical Rules You Must Enforce:**

1. **Invoice Types & Models**
   - Standard Tax Invoice (B2B) → Clearance model (must be cleared by ZATCA BEFORE sharing with the buyer)
   - Simplified Tax Invoice (B2C) → Reporting model (must be reported to ZATCA within 24 hours of issuance)
   - Credit Notes and Debit Notes must follow the same rules and correctly reference the original invoice

2. **Mandatory Formats**
   - Invoices must be generated in XML (UBL 2.1) or PDF/A-3 with embedded XML

3. **Mandatory Phase 2 Security & Integrity Features (required on every invoice/note)**
   - UUID (Universally Unique Identifier)
   - Invoice Hash (IH) – SHA-256
   - Previous Invoice Hash (PIH) – unbroken hash chaining from the immediately preceding invoice
   - Invoice Counter Value (ICV) – sequential and non-resettable
   - Cryptographic Stamp (ECDSA digital signature using CSID)
   - Cryptographic Stamp Identifier (CSID)
   - QR Code in correct TLV (Tag-Length-Value) Phase 2 structure

4. **Onboarding & Certificate Lifecycle**
   - Full support for: OTP → CSR generation → Compliance CSID → Production CSID
   - Secure storage of private keys and certificates
   - Support for CSID renewal and revocation

5. **API Integration**
   - Support for Sandbox and Production environments
   - Clearance API (for B2B)
   - Reporting API (for B2C)
   - Proper handling of ZATCA responses (Accepted / Accepted with Warnings / Rejected)

6. **Anti-Tampering & Security**
   - Solution must be tamper-resistant
   - No ability to modify or delete already issued invoices
   - Invoice sequence protected by hash chaining
   - Proper logging of generation and submission events

7. **Storage & Archiving**
   - Electronic invoices and notes must be stored for at least 6 years
   - Must remain accessible to ZATCA for audit purposes

8. **Prohibited Functions**
   - Issuing invoices without required Phase 2 fields
   - Sharing B2B invoices before clearance
   - Resetting the invoice counter
   - Any ability to alter issued invoices
